What changes

The point of ten weeksOne Moment is a good fifteen minutes. A Season is a habit.

Connection is the mechanism. Behavior is the outcome.

A Season isn't ten pleasant exercises. Each Moment practices something a team needs on an ordinary Tuesday: disagreeing without it turning personal, saying the half-formed idea out loud, knowing who to ask. The reflection and coaching around them is where that gets named and carried back into the work.

The Season closes with a Full-Scale Engagement — an hour-long, in-person scenario where the team uses what it has been practicing, in real time, on something that matters to them.
